The Case of the Missing Heart

A secret case has arrived, Detective Wijdane.

One heart has disappeared. Three clues remain. Your mission is to discover where it went.

Case File No. 001

Suspect: Someone who smiles whenever your name appears.

Evidence: A thousand little moments, a warm feeling, and a message that has never been fully explained.

Clue 1: The Message 📜

The missing heart was last seen near someone whose presence can make an ordinary day feel special.

What should a good detective follow?

Not quite, Detective! 🕵️

Footprints disappear, but meaningful moments can stay in the heart. Look a little closer.

Clue 2: The Witness 🌙

A witness reports that the suspect never needed a special occasion to appreciate Wijdane. Even the little things about her mattered.

What is the strongest evidence of appreciation?

Look beyond the obvious 💌

Gifts can be lovely, but sincere appreciation comes from the heart.

Clue 3: The Final Question 🔐

The last note says: “The missing heart was never stolen. It simply found someone worth appreciating.”

Who could have written this secret note?

💗

CASE CLOSED

Dear Wijdane,

If you have reached this page, you have solved the mystery. But the most important discovery is still waiting for you.

I made this little case for you because I wanted to remind you that you are appreciated more than a simple message can say.

Your presence, your personality, and the little things that make you who you are deserve to be appreciated. Thank you for being yourself and for bringing your own kind of light into the world.

I hope this little surprise makes you smile, because you deserve kind words and beautiful moments.

With appreciation,

Mohamed ❤️

Some mysteries end with an answer. This one ends with a heartfelt message.
